mercy). **Kept as authored.** The owner may want "I fled the fight." if the wording ought to be
undeniable, at the cost of making his worthiness harder to see (D25, matrix).

## Readability findings

- **No clipped or unreadable line at 1:1.** Several lines sit flush against the panel edge (Brand
  C1 and W1, Sigvaldi C2 and C3, Hakon C1 and W3, Egil C4, Ragna W2). They are exactly at D50's
  132 px cap. **Parked:** a 1–2 px margin would need a tighter cap and some rewording.
- **Every hover label matches a visible mark or a deliberate absence.** The smallest hit areas are
  10×8 and 10×10, inside D5's minimum of 8×8.
- **Unlabeled marks:** a glint on Grim's face, and frost speckle on returned Hakon (his identity
  layer, D5). **Parked to the art:** Grim's glint could be read as evidence, so remove it in the
  next art pass.
- **The sword in a back-view hand and the lower blade have no hover area.** Accepted: the hand's
  fact ("sword in hand") carries it.
- **Kolbeinn's C4, "My wife will tell you."**, promises a witness who never comes, since day 1 has
  none. Accepted as flavour; it is part of his lie.
- **Guided souls (Ulf, Kolbeinn, Hallr) outline every hit area; later souls show corner pips.**
  Intended: it is the mentor's guidance (D16).
