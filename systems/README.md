# systems/

Scene-scoped coordinators that connect several entities; never global, never autoloaded.

| System | Role |
|---|---|
| `desk/` | `DeskSystem` connects the desk's entities to `DeskCommands` (the only command layer, D13), seats each soul, runs the clock (which waits for the tale, D5) and owns its helpers: `DeskPins` (the pin gesture and its outcome: a thread drawn in, a thread already made, a no-seam pin's tick, gutter and slack drop, D8, D12, D36), `DeskHighlights` (the selection, the lit pair, a held soul's pair, the mentor's highlight, D28, D48), `DeskHuginn` (knot clicks and asking the raven, D29, D33, D40) and `DeskMentor` (her guided step written in the bubble, which holds the gate while it writes, and her comment again, D18). It also keeps the hover tags off the sheets' headings and the note, and as far as it can off the sheets (D20), and sets each day's gate band and doors (D39). `DeskTelling` seats the sheets and tells the soul's claim, then its witness, behind one consumed click (D4, D7); `DeskNote` builds the note's Captions (quoted pairs, marks), lights the pair a caption quotes, lights the named door's glyph while a law caption shows, and holds the gate while a caption is written (D10, D28, D43). `StampFlow` owns the stamp: prompt, drag, confirm, the thud, the held soul (D9, D48); `StampConsequences` lands what it leaves: the citation and her comment on the note, the feather's fall and the tide's step at the impact (D16, D38). `PresentationGate` owns "a presentation is in progress": it decides each press (consumed, passed or ignored in the slam), pauses and cancels the presentation clocks (ADR-0009); `ThreadBoard` draws the threads and keeps a slack thread's drop off the papers; `DeskReadouts` reads doors, NEW marks and the HUD; `PhaseFlow` keeps the place in the dawn, dusk and ending screens and draws them on Odin's writ and the day-book (`show_screens`; its `card()` is what the text harness prints, D44); `DeskScreens` wires the writ and the book to the gate and the sounds (the horn with the dark card, the page turn, the drone while the book is open) and turns their buttons into commands; `HuginnAsk`, `PinSelection` and `ThreadRouter` are pure helpers |
| `caption_strip/` | The clerk's note: the bottom edge of a parchment page with a speaker mark (`NoteMarks`), up to two lines, the message priority (D33) and the quill's written reveal; `Caption` is its typed message (D10, D43) |

A system holds references to the entities it coordinates (set in the owning scene) and connects
their signals itself.
